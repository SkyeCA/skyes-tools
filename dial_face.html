Pivot & Arm
Start/end angle stay fixed — this only lifts the two ends of the printed arc toward the center, flattening the curve without changing where the needle points.
Scale Range
0° = straight up. Negative = counter-clockwise (left), positive = clockwise (right). This is the needle's true angular range — it sets where min/max values point. Use "Scale curvature" (under Pivot & Arm) to change how curved the printed arc looks without affecting this.
